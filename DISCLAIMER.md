> S. Maharjan, Q. Zhu, Y. Zhang, S. Gjessing, T. Başar, "Dependable Demand
> Response Management in the Smart Grid: A Stackelberg Game Approach," *IEEE
> Transactions on Smart Grid*, vol. 4, no. 1, pp. 120–132, March 2013.
> DOI: 10.1109/TSG.2012.2223766

It is **not affiliated with, endorsed by, or reviewed by** the paper's authors,
Simula Research Laboratory, the University of Oslo, or the University of Illinois.

- All code here is an independent implementation of the paper's published
  equations; no text, figures, or code from the paper are reproduced.
- All numerical results in this repository are produced by this implementation.
  Where they are compared to the paper, the paper's values were digitized by eye
  from its figures and are labeled as such. **Nothing here should be cited as the
  paper's results.**

  error in the paper's analysis.
